PLANET 07

URANUS

A cold blue-green ice giant with a strange rotation and faint rings.

OVERVIEW

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and the third-largest planet by diameter. It is an ice giant made mostly of hydrogen, helium, and materials such as water, methane, and ammonia. Its blue-green color comes mainly from methane in its atmosphere.

ATMOSPHERE & RINGS

Uranus has a cold atmosphere with clouds and powerful winds. It also has a system of faint rings that are difficult to see. The planet's atmosphere contains methane, which absorbs red light and gives Uranus its blue-green color.

TEMPERATURE

Uranus is one of the coldest planets in our solar system. Temperatures in its upper atmosphere can reach about -224°C (-371°F). Its great distance from the Sun makes it a very cold world.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Uranus takes about 84 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun. It rotates once in about 17 hours. Uranus is unusual because its axis is tilted about 98 degrees, causing it to rotate almost on its side.

EXPLORATION

Uranus has only been visited closely by one spacecraft, Voyager 2, which flew past the planet in 1986. The mission discovered new information about Uranus, including its atmosphere, rings, magnetic field, and several moons.
