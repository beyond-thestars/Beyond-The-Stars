01 / THE COSMOS

THE
COSMOS

The cosmos includes everything that exists: space, time, matter, energy, stars, planets, galaxies and everything beyond them.

Scientists study the cosmos to understand how the universe began, how it changes and what may exist beyond what we can observe.

Although humans have learned a great deal, the universe still contains many mysteries waiting to be discovered.

THE UNIVERSE SPACE • TIME • MATTER • ENERGY

02 / GALAXIES

GALAXIES

Galaxies are enormous systems containing stars, planets, gas, dust and other objects held together by gravity.

Our home galaxy is the Milky Way. It contains billions of stars, including our Sun, and is only one of countless galaxies in the observable universe.

Galaxies can have different shapes and sizes, including spiral, elliptical and irregular forms.

OUR GALAXY THE MILKY WAY

03 / NEBULAE

NEBULAE

Nebulae are enormous clouds of gas and dust found throughout space.

Some nebulae are regions where new stars are forming, while others are created from material released by dying stars.

Their incredible shapes and colors make nebulae some of the most fascinating structures observed by astronomers.

COSMIC CLOUDS GAS • DUST • STAR FORMATION

04 / BLACK HOLES

BLACK
HOLES

Black holes are regions of space where gravity is extremely strong. Their gravitational pull is so powerful that light cannot escape once it passes the event horizon.

They can form when massive stars collapse at the end of their lives. Scientists also study supermassive black holes found at the centers of many galaxies.

Even though black holes cannot be seen directly, scientists can observe their effects on nearby matter and light.
