PLANET 05

JUPITER

The largest planet in our solar system, known for its giant storms and many moons.

OVERVIEW

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter does not have a solid surface like Earth or Mars.

ATMOSPHERE & STORMS

Jupiter has a thick atmosphere with colorful bands of clouds. The planet is famous for the Great Red Spot, a huge storm that has lasted for many years. Jupiter also has very strong winds and powerful storms.

TEMPERATURE

Jupiter is very cold in its upper atmosphere, with temperatures around -110°C (-166°F). However, temperatures become much hotter deep inside the planet because of the enormous pressure.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Jupiter takes about 12 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun. It rotates very quickly, completing one rotation in about 10 hours. This fast rotation causes Jupiter to have a slightly flattened shape.

EXPLORATION

Jupiter has been explored by several spacecraft. The Galileo mission studied Jupiter and its moons for many years. The Juno spacecraft is also studying Jupiter's atmosphere, magnetic field, gravity, and interior to help scientists understand more about this giant planet.

DID YOU KNOW?

Jupiter is so large that more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it by volume.

Jupiter has a faint ring system that is difficult to see from Earth.
