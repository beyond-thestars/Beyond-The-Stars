PLANET 06

SATURN

A giant planet famous for its beautiful rings and many moons.

OVERVIEW

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Saturn is best known for its large and bright system of rings.

ATMOSPHERE & RINGS

Saturn has a thick atmosphere with bands of clouds and strong winds. Its rings are made mostly of pieces of ice and rock. The rings are divided into many sections and extend far around the planet.

TEMPERATURE

Saturn is a very cold planet. The temperature near the top of its clouds is around -140°C (-220°F). Deeper inside the planet, pressure and temperature increase greatly.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Saturn takes about 29.5 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun. It rotates very quickly, taking about 10.7 hours to complete one rotation. Like Jupiter, its fast rotation gives Saturn a slightly flattened shape.

EXPLORATION

Saturn has been visited by several spacecraft. The Cassini mission studied Saturn, its rings, and its moons for many years. Cassini provided important information about the planet and helped scientists learn more about worlds such as Titan and Enceladus.

DID YOU KNOW?

Saturn is less dense than water. If there were a bathtub large enough, Saturn would float.
