PLANET 02

VENUS

A hot, cloudy world with the thickest atmosphere of all the rocky planets.

OVERVIEW

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is similar in size and structure to Earth. However, its environment is very different. Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system because its thick atmosphere traps heat.

SURFACE & ATMOSPHERE

Venus has a rocky surface with mountains, large plains, and many volcanoes. Its atmosphere is mostly made of carbon dioxide, with clouds of sulfuric acid. The thick atmosphere creates extremely strong pressure at the surface.

TEMPERATURE

Venus has an average surface temperature of about 465°C (869°F). Its thick atmosphere creates a powerful greenhouse effect that keeps the planet extremely hot, even at night.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Venus takes about 225 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun. It rotates very slowly, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation. Venus also rotates in the opposite direction from most planets.

EXPLORATION

Venus has been visited by many spacecraft. The Soviet Venera missions were the first to successfully land spacecraft on its surface and send information back to Earth. More recent missions have studied its atmosphere and surface to better understand how Venus became so different from Earth.

DID YOU KNOW?

Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.

Venus is sometimes called Earth's "sister planet" because it is similar in size and structure.
