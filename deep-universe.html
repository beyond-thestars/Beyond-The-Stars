DEEP UNIVERSE

Discover distant galaxies, mysterious black holes, beautiful nebulae and the wonders hidden beyond our Solar System. Explore the science, beauty and mysteries of the vast universe.

01

Cosmos

The cosmos includes everything that exists: space, time, matter and energy. It contains billions of galaxies, stars, planets and other celestial objects. Scientists study the cosmos to understand how the universe began, how it changes over time and what mysteries may still be waiting to be discovered. The universe continues to expand, revealing new questions about its origin and future.

02

Galaxies

Galaxies are enormous systems made up of stars, planets, gas, dust and dark matter, all held together by gravity. They come in different shapes, including spiral, elliptical and irregular galaxies. Our Solar System belongs to the Milky Way, a spiral galaxy containing billions of stars. By studying distant galaxies, astronomers can learn how these systems form, grow and change throughout cosmic history.

03

Nebulae

Nebulae are enormous clouds of gas and dust found throughout space. Some form when stars reach the end of their lives, while others provide the conditions needed for new stars to form. These clouds can display beautiful colors and complex shapes when observed with powerful telescopes. Nebulae help astronomers understand how stars are born and how matter is recycled throughout the universe.

04

Black Holes

Black holes are regions of space where gravity is so strong that nothing can escape from inside the event horizon, not even light. Many form when massive stars collapse at the end of their lives. Supermassive black holes also exist at the centers of many galaxies. Although black holes cannot be seen directly, scientists study their effects on nearby stars, gas and light to understand these extraordinary objects.
