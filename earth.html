PLANET 03

EARTH

Our home planet and the only known world where life exists.

OVERVIEW

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the largest of the four rocky planets. It has liquid water, a protective atmosphere, and the right conditions for life. Earth is home to millions of different plants, animals, and other forms of life.

SURFACE & ATMOSPHERE

Earth has oceans, continents, mountains, deserts, forests, and many other types of environments. About 71% of its surface is covered by water. Its atmosphere is mostly nitrogen and oxygen and helps protect life from harmful radiation.

TEMPERATURE

Earth has an average surface temperature of about 15°C (59°F). Its atmosphere helps keep temperatures within a range where liquid water can exist. Temperatures vary greatly depending on location, season, and weather.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Earth takes about 365.25 days to complete one orbit around the Sun. It rotates once about every 24 hours, creating day and night. Earth's axis is tilted about 23.5 degrees, which helps create the different seasons.

EXPLORATION

Earth has been studied from space by thousands of satellites and spacecraft. These missions help scientists understand weather, climate, oceans, land, and changes happening on our planet. Earth observation also helps us prepare for natural events and protect the environment.
