PLANET 01

MERCURY

The closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system.

OVERVIEW

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system. It is a rocky planet with a surface covered by craters, mountains, and large plains. Mercury has no rings and no moons.

SURFACE & ATMOSPHERE

Mercury's surface looks similar to Earth's Moon because it has many impact craters. Its atmosphere is extremely thin, so it cannot hold much heat. This causes very large temperature changes between daytime and nighttime.

TEMPERATURE

Mercury can become extremely hot during the day and very cold at night. Temperatures can reach about 430°C (800°F) during the day and fall to about -180°C (-290°F) at night.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Mercury travels around the Sun faster than any other planet. One year on Mercury lasts only 88 Earth days. However, one complete rotation of Mercury takes about 59 Earth days. Because of this unusual relationship, a solar day on Mercury lasts about 176 Earth days.

EXPLORATION

Scientists have studied Mercury with spacecraft such as Mariner 10 and MESSENGER. These missions helped scientists learn more about Mercury's surface, magnetic field, atmosphere, and interior. More recently, the BepiColombo mission has been studying the planet to help scientists understand its history and formation.
