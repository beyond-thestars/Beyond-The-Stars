PLANET 04

MARS

The Red Planet, a cold rocky world with giant volcanoes and deep valleys.

OVERVIEW

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is known as the Red Planet because of its reddish color. It is a rocky planet with a thin atmosphere and a surface covered with craters, mountains, valleys, and large plains.

SURFACE & ATMOSPHERE

Mars has a rocky and dusty surface. It is home to Olympus Mons, the largest volcano in the solar system, and Valles Marineris, a huge system of canyons. Its atmosphere is very thin and is made mostly of carbon dioxide.

TEMPERATURE

Mars is a cold planet with an average temperature of about -63°C (-81°F). Temperatures can change greatly between day and night, and some areas can become much colder during winter.

ORBIT & ROTATION

Mars takes about 687 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun. It rotates once every 24.6 hours, so a day on Mars is very similar in length to a day on Earth. Mars also has seasons because its axis is tilted.

EXPLORATION

Mars has been explored by many spacecraft, including orbiters, landers, and rovers. Missions such as Curiosity and Perseverance have studied its rocks, soil, atmosphere, and ancient environments. Scientists continue to search for evidence that Mars may have once had conditions suitable for life.
